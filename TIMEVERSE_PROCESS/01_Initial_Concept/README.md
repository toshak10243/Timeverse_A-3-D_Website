# 01 · Initial Concept & Project Proposal

## 1. Project Overview: TIMEVERSE
**TIMEVERSE** is an interactive, browser-based 3D digital museum and temporal portal. Rather than presenting historical and speculative timeline data as flat encyclopedia entries, TIMEVERSE places the user inside interactive spatial environments spanning ancient, pre-modern, industrial, and speculative futures.

### Core Creative Premise
- The user assumes the role of a temporal explorer stepping through an anomalous Chrono-Portal.
- The interface balances cinematic video backgrounds, ambient procedural soundscapes, and real-time Three.js spatial scenes with orbital camera navigation.
- Every era features authentic historical markers and clickable focal artifacts that reveal contextual knowledge and architectural details.

## 2. Selected Historical & Speculative Eras
1. **Ancient Egypt (c. 2560 BCE)**: The construction of the Giza necropolis, the Khufu Great Pyramid, Sphinx, and solar symbolism.
2. **Medieval Europe (c. 1250 CE)**: High Gothic fortification, stone keeps, knight heraldry, feudal agrarian life, and defensive ramparts.
3. **Industrial Revolution (c. 1840 CE)**: Steam-powered locomotives, textile mill machinery, coal-fired atmospheric smokestacks, and urban mechanization.
4. **Neo-Metropolis Speculative Future (c. 2200 CE)**: Floating arcologies, vertical green towers, quantum energy spires, and autonomous sky-transport conduits.

## 3. Interaction Mechanics & Aesthetic Goals
- **Atmospheric Palette**: Dark, high-contrast, modern glassmorphic UI with vibrant neon accentuation (`#5ee7ff`, `#f59e0b`, `#60a5fa`, `#f97316`, `#a855f7`).
- **Smooth Temporal Transitions**: Screen-space warp tunnels with dynamic radial blur and sound frequency shifts during era transitions.
- **Dual Media Types**:
  - High-framerate cinematic Kling AI video backdrops.
  - Interactive 3D WebGL / Three.js geometry with orbital inspection and focus points.
  - Multi-layered procedural spatial audio synthesis (Web Audio API) paired with generative ambient tracks.
