## 2. Historical & Architectural References
- **Giza Plateau**: Architectural elevations of the Great Pyramid of Khufu (base 230m, 51.5° slope) and orientation along cardinal points.
- **Bodiam Castle & Krak des Chevaliers**: Concentric defensive structures, machicolations, arrow slits, and timber drawbridges.
- **Manchester & Ironbridge Gorge (1840s)**: Stephenson's Rocket locomotive configurations, brick boiler chimneys, and iron truss spans.
- **Speculative Arcology (2200 CE)**: Syd Mead and Solarpunk design motifs — integration of bio-domes, photovoltaic facades, and magnetic levitation rail lines.

## 3. UI/UX Reference Systems
- Minimalist science fiction HUD interfaces (Figma layout prototypes).
- Glassmorphic translucent frosted panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with hairline white borders to maintain immersion without occluding 3D viewports.
